Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

GlassPost lets you schedule Instagram posts. This policy explains what we collect and why.

Data we collect

  • Your Instagram user ID, username, account type and profile picture, provided when you connect your account.
  • An access token that authorises GlassPost to publish on your behalf. It is encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM).
  • The media files, captions and publish times you submit for scheduling.

How we use it

Solely to publish the posts you schedule and to show your queue. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone other than our infrastructure providers (hosting, database and media storage) acting on our behalf.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account is connected. Removing GlassPost in Instagram, or requesting deletion, removes your account record, tokens and scheduled posts. Uploaded media files are removed on request. See data deletion.

Contact

Questions or requests: privacy@your-domain.com.
